# NASWEO Institutional Design System Record (DESIGN.md)

## 1. Product Truth & Context
- **Institution**: NARL Staff Welfare Organization (NASWEO)
- **Host Body**: National Agricultural Research Laboratories (NARL), Kabete, Nairobi, Kenya
- **Scope**: ~300 Members (Agricultural Research Scientists and Support Staff)
- **Visitor Mode**: **Operate & Read** — High-integrity financial ledger, checkoff reconciliation, amortized credit schedules, and statutory retirement tracking.

---

## 2. Visual Direction: "Institutional Research & Welfare Ledger"
Unlike generic AI-generated templates (which rely on saturated dark-mode backgrounds, neon gradient orbs, 24px over-rounded pills, and decorative empty cards), NASWEO uses an authoritative Commonwealth research institution visual language:

### Typography
- **Primary Interface**: Plus Jakarta Sans / Inter with optical tracking (`tracking-tight` on headings, uppercase kicker labels with `text-[10px]` or `text-[11px]`).
- **Tabular Figures & Ledgers**: IBM Plex Mono / monospace with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` for all monetary quantities (KES), payroll numbers, transaction hashes, and percentages.

### Color Hierarchy
- **Brand Forest & Cypress Green**:
  - Sidebar & Masthead: `#0F291E` / `#091D15`
  - Border Accents: `#1B4332` / `#2D6A4F`
  - Positive / Equity Tokens: `#065F46` / `#D1FAE5`
- **Surface Canvas**:
  - Background: `#F8FAFC` (Calm off-white/paper slate)
  - Cards: Crisp `#FFFFFF` with hairline `#E2E8F0` borders
- **Semantic Signals**:
  - Arrears / Default: `#991B1B` on `#FEF2F2` (hairline `#FCA5A5`)
  - Retirement Horizon Attention: `#92400E` on `#FFFBEB` (hairline `#FCD34D`)
  - Scientific / Audit Metadata: Slate `#334155` on `#F1F5F9`

---

## 3. Two Reading Speeds
1. **Executive Path**: High-level balance sheet metrics (Share Capital, Benevolence Reserve, Total Credit Exposure) presented in a unified partitioned ledger strip.
2. **Audit Path**: High-density tabular ledgers featuring exact posting dates, transaction references, checkoff reconciliation states, and amortization schedules.
